# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people across language levels, backgrounds, identities, and experience. Critique work without attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized language or imagery.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and project-related public spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary.

Until a maintainer contact is published, report conduct issues through GitHub's private repository contact options rather than a public issue. The launch checklist requires a confirmed private channel before the repository is made public.

Maintainers should protect reporter privacy, consider context and impact, and communicate proportionate actions.
